Credit Card Fraud Detection

          
Compares logistic regression, XGBoost and an unsupervised Isolation Forest on 284,807 transactions, scored on a later time period. Sets the alert threshold by dollar cost.

A/B Test: Mobile Game Gate

          
Analyzes a 90,189-player experiment with a sample-ratio check, z-tests, bootstrap, a Bayesian model and power analysis.
